# Code of conduct

WaveBench follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at kilic_kursat@hotmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially.
